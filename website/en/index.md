## The building blocks for your product

<div class="features"><article><h3>Enroll devices your way</h3><p>Create private, single-use QR codes for configured IP or domain addresses. Kylok binds each enrollment to its management scope.</p></article><article><h3>Deliver apps and confirm the result</h3><p>Assign signed APKs, resume interrupted downloads and verify the installed package, version and signing identity before reporting success.</p></article><article><h3>Apply policies you can observe</h3><p>Manage required apps, Android Lock Task, runtime permissions and selected restrictions. See the observed outcome for each rule.</p></article><article><h3>Fit the stack you already own</h3><p>Integrate public Go and Android APIs, start with durable SQLite/filesystem storage, and keep authorization and lifecycle in your host.</p></article></div>

## See Kylok in action

The example web console walks through enrollment, APK assignment and policy changes, then shows device and operation results. The Android dashboard separates requested state from what the device has actually installed or applied. Use the examples to evaluate the SDK flow; your product owns its management experience.
